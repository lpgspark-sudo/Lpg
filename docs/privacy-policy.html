Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LPG ("we", "our", "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application to book LPG cylinders (domestic, commercial, and industrial).

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details: Mobile number (used for OTP login and order updates)
  • Delivery address: Address you provide or your device location, used to deliver your order
  • Order details: Cylinder type, size, quantity, and order history
  • Payment information: Processed securely via Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details on our servers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your cylinder booking
  • To contact you regarding order status, delivery, or support
  • To process the advance booking/service charge via Razorpay
  • To improve our service and respond to complaints

3. Sharing of Information

We share only the necessary order details (name, phone, address, cylinder type/quantity) with our LPG dealer/distributor network to fulfil your order. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Payment Processing

Payments are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. Razorpay's own privacy policy governs how your payment data is handled during transactions.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Location Access

We request location access only to help auto-fill your delivery address. You may also enter your address manually without granting location permission.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
